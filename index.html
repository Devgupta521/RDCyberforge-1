01 / ABOUT US

Curiosity is the first
line of defence.

RD CyberForge is a learning-focused cybersecurity initiative for students, beginners, and anyone curious about digital security.

We make cybersecurity approachable through structured training, practical workshops, capture-the-flag (CTF) challenges, and community events. Our goal is to help learners build strong foundations, practice responsibly, and keep exploring.

A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER
FOUNDER

G. Devendra

Building a community where curiosity becomes knowledge and knowledge becomes responsible action.

LEARN. EXPLORE. SECURE.

Building a community.
Creating opportunities.

I started RD CyberForge with a simple thought: cybersecurity learning should be accessible to everyone, especially students and beginners who are eager to explore the field but don't know where to start.

My vision is to create a community where learners can develop practical skills, participate in workshops and CTFs, collaborate on projects, and prepare for future opportunities in cybersecurity.

I believe that learning together, sharing knowledge, and practising responsibly can help us build a stronger cybersecurity community. This is just the beginning of our journey.

G. Devendra Founder, RD CyberForge
