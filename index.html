01 / ABOUT US

Curiosity is the first
line of defence.

RD CyberForge is a learning-focused cybersecurity initiative created to help students, beginners, and aspiring security professionals build practical cybersecurity skills.

We believe cybersecurity education should be accessible, practical, and community-driven. Through structured training, hands-on workshops, Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, technical projects, and community events, we encourage learners to build strong foundations, practice responsibly, and keep exploring.

Our mission is to create opportunities for learners to understand cybersecurity, collaborate with others, develop technical confidence, and prepare for future opportunities in the cybersecurity field.

I started RD CyberForge with a simple thought: cybersecurity learning should be accessible to everyone, especially students and beginners who are eager to explore the field but don't know where to start.

My vision is to create a community where learners can develop practical skills, participate in workshops and CTFs, collaborate on projects, and prepare for future opportunities in cybersecurity.

I believe that learning together, sharing knowledge, and practising responsibly can help us build a stronger cybersecurity community. This is just the beginning of our journey.
