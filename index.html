01 / ABOUT US

Curiosity is the first
line of defence.

RD CyberForge is a learning-focused cybersecurity initiative created to help students, beginners, and aspiring security professionals build practical cybersecurity skills.

We believe cybersecurity education should be accessible, practical, and community-driven. Through structured training, hands-on workshops, Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, technical projects, and community events, we encourage learners to build strong foundations, practice responsibly, and keep exploring.

Our mission is to create opportunities for learners to understand cybersecurity, collaborate with others, develop technical confidence, and prepare for future opportunities in the cybersecurity field.

Everything you need to know about RD CyberForge, our mission, organisation and cybersecurity activities.

01 About RD CyberForge

RD CyberForge is a student-led cybersecurity initiative and technology organisation focused on practical learning, cybersecurity awareness, skill development and community collaboration.

RD CyberForge began its journey in September 2026, with the vision of creating opportunities for practical cybersecurity learning and collaboration.

Learn: Build strong technical foundations.

Explore: Gain practical experience through labs, workshops and challenges.

Secure: Develop the skills and mindset to contribute to a safer digital environment.

Our vision is to build a learning-driven cybersecurity community that encourages practical skills, innovation, collaboration and responsible technology use.

02 Founders & Organisation

RD CyberForge was founded by G. Devendra, along with co-founders Sagala Rajyalakshmi and C. Gowthami, with the aim of creating a platform for students and cybersecurity enthusiasts to learn, explore and grow.

RD CyberForge is a student-led cybersecurity and technology initiative operating as a sole proprietorship and registered under the MSME/Udyam framework.

No. RD CyberForge is an independent initiative, not a government department or government-owned organisation. MSME/Udyam registration does not automatically mean government accreditation of its training programs.

RD CyberForge aims to collaborate with educational institutions, cybersecurity communities, technology organisations and industry professionals to create learning and development opportunities.

03 Mission & Activities

Our main motive is to make cybersecurity learning more accessible and practical by encouraging hands-on experience, technical exploration, problem-solving and knowledge sharing.

We organise cybersecurity training programs, Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions, technical workshops, practical learning activities and community initiatives.

Yes. We encourage hands-on learning through practical exercises, cybersecurity labs, CTF challenges, workshops and technical activities.
